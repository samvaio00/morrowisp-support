Morrowisp press kit

Three daily logic challenges. One quiet garden.

The game

Morrowisp asks you to predict how weather actions change ceramic stones, find a missing action, or build a sequence that reaches a target garden. The reveal runs the rules step by step, showing why the result happened.

A daily journey has three rounds and a clear ending. Six free chapters introduce the rules, while Drift, practice and the Workshop offer more to explore. Pieces earned through play let you arrange your own ceramic garden.

Facts at a glance

  • Developer: Mahesh Kommu
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad; iOS / iPadOS 16 or later
  • First released: 20 October 2026
  • Free download; all six chapters and daily puzzles are free
  • Occasional between-round ads in some modes; never during a puzzle. Chapter 1, daily and archive puzzles are ad-free.
  • Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase; see the App Store for your local price
  • Instructions and written help: English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Puzzle labels and the rest of the interface remain English.
