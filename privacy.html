This policy explains how Morrowisp handles device progress, optional backups, purchases, advertising, and information you choose to send.

Progress and play records

Morrowisp stores your garden, puzzle progress, results, settings, and recovery copies on your device. Workshop arrangements are processed locally while you use the Workshop. Local play records can include event timestamps and coarse play results. These records help the app keep progress and describe play activity.

The current iOS release has no configured endpoint for automatically sending these gameplay records to the developer. Local play records are separate from the data processed by the advertising services described below. You do not need a Morrowisp account to play.

Optional iCloud snapshots

On supported iPhones and iPads, you can enable iCloud backups and manually choose Back up now. This sends a garden snapshot to a private document container in your Apple iCloud storage. It is not automatic synchronization and does not send the snapshot to a developer-operated server or a public database.

Ordinary progress saves remain on your device. Restoring an iCloud snapshot requires your action and a review in the app. Turning off the backup option stops future app backup actions; it does not delete a snapshot already held by Apple. Apple’s account, storage, and privacy controls govern that copy. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Apple purchases

Apple processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Morrowisp reads the verified purchase entitlement on your device to decide whether ads should be suppressed. The app does not operate its own payment form or send payment details or purchase receipts to a developer server or to Google’s advertising SDK.

Apple manages billing and purchase history under its own terms and privacy practices. Restoring a garden does not grant or remove your Apple purchases.

Advertising and consent

The advertising-supported iOS app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform for between-round ads and consent choices. These services can process IP-derived approximate location, device and app identifiers, advertising and product interactions, crash reports, performance information, and other diagnostic data.

That information may be used to provide and measure advertising, operate consent features, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google’s processing is separate from Morrowisp’s local gameplay records. Ads are configured without personalization, and Morrowisp does not request Apple tracking permission. Google can still process the data described above.

Google consent information gates ad requests. Where required by Google’s consent system, you can reopen Ad privacy choices in Settings to review or change available choices. A verified Remove Ads entitlement suppresses between-round advertising; buying it does not itself erase information already processed by a service.

Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps. Google also explains its ad controls; those account and browser controls are separate from the in-app consent choices and may not apply to every app ad.

Exports and support messages

Copying or sharing a garden or local play-data export is your choice. It goes to the clipboard or the destination you select; the app does not automatically upload it to support. An export may contain your garden and play history, so review it before sharing.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include, and uses them to respond and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment details. You can ask about or request removal of support correspondence using the contact below.

Storage, recovery, and deletion

Local progress and play records remain on the device until removed through the relevant app or system storage controls. Reset draft progress starts a fresh garden but retains a local recovery backup; it is not an erase-all-data command. Other local records and Apple purchases can remain.

Deleting the app and its data, or clearing the web preview’s site data, removes that installation’s local records. Offloading an iOS app is different from deleting its data. Exports, device or system backups, and an existing iCloud snapshot can remain separately. Use Apple’s iCloud storage controls to manage or delete the cloud copy; deleting local progress or switching off app backups does not delete it.

Google and Apple retain information they process under their own policies and controls. Contact support with questions about information you have sent directly to the developer. Support correspondence is kept as needed to respond and resolve your request or meet legal obligations; you can request its deletion.

These support pages

These static pages contain no added analytics script, advertising SDK, or contact form. They are hosted with GitHub Pages, whose service may process request metadata such as your IP address for delivery and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to that service’s own privacy practices.
